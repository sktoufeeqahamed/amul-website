Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the shelf life of Amul Kool Rose bottle? expand_more

Amul Kool Rose has a shelf life of 6 months from the date of manufacture when stored in a cool and dry place. Once opened, consume immediately or refrigerate at 4°C and finish within 24 hours.

Does it contain any artificial chemical preservatives? expand_more

No. Amul Kool utilizes Ultra High Temperature (UHT) continuous sterilization and aseptic glass bottle capping, eliminating the need for synthetic food preservatives while preserving taste and freshness.

What makes Amul Rose flavor different from artificial syrups? expand_more

Our flavor extract is derived directly from steam-distilled Damascus rose petal extracts, imparting natural botanical floral notes without the harsh, cloying aftertaste of synthetic flavor drops.

Is this safe for lactose sensitive or vegetarian diets? expand_more

Amul Kool is 100% vegetarian (Green Dot certified). It contains standardized natural cow & buffalo dairy milk, so individuals with strict lactose intolerance should consume according to their personal dietary guidelines.
